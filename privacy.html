Privacy Policy

Application: “Digital Me” · Operator: Aetheris (Hongkong) Limited · Last updated: 29 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the “Digital Me” application (the “Application”), operated by Aetheris (Hongkong) Limited and its affiliated entities (“Aetheris”), handles data. The Application is a private internal tool and is not offered to the general public.

1. Scope

The Application is used solely by Aetheris to administer the accounting and financial records of entities that Aetheris owns or is authorized to manage. It does not collect data from, or provide services to, members of the public.

2. Data We Access

When connected to Intuit QuickBooks Online with the account owner’s authorization, the Application accesses accounting data such as company financial reports, invoices, bills, customers, vendors, and transactions, for the authorized company file(s) only. Access is read-oriented and limited to the scopes granted during authorization.

3. How Data Is Used

Accessed data is used exclusively for Aetheris’ internal bookkeeping, reporting, treasury, and analysis. It is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party for marketing or any other purpose.

4. Authorization Tokens

OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Intuit are stored locally in the operator’s own controlled environment and are used only to maintain the authorized connection. Authorization can be revoked at any time from the Intuit account, after which the Application can no longer access the data.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data and credentials are kept within Aetheris’ own controlled systems. Aetheris applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect them. The Application runs in a private environment rather than a publicly exposed server.

6. Data Retention

Financial data is retained only as long as necessary for Aetheris’ internal accounting and statutory record-keeping obligations.

7. Third-Party Platforms

The Application relies on Intuit’s QuickBooks Online API. Intuit’s own privacy practices are governed by Intuit’s privacy policy. This policy covers only Aetheris’ handling of data within the Application.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

9. Contact

Questions regarding this policy may be directed to jett@aetheris.hk.
